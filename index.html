About Me

From civil engineering roots to quality leadership in enterprise software.

I began my professional journey as a Civil Engineer after graduating from JNTU Hyderabad. While working on construction projects, I discovered a much stronger passion for technology, automation, and software engineering.

During the COVID period, I dedicated myself to learning software testing, ETL testing, and industry best practices, successfully transitioning into the IT industry.

Since then, I have grown from an ETL Test Engineer into a Senior Quality Engineer specializing in test automation, performance engineering, AI-assisted testing, digital twin platforms, and enterprise software quality. In my current role at Rockwell Automation, I lead quality initiatives for industrial IoT and edge management products.

I enjoy solving complex engineering challenges, designing scalable automation frameworks, improving software reliability, mentoring teammates, and continuously learning emerging technologies. My strengths include leadership, client communication, root cause analysis, and quality engineering excellence.
